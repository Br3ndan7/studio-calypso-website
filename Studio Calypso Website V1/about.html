Her story

Thirty years at the bench

Over more than three decades at the jeweller's bench, Hestelle has learned exactly what silver and gold will and won't do: how fine a ring can be and still last a lifetime, how a stone wants to sit, how a clasp should feel the thousandth time it's opened.

That knowledge shows up in the small things. Edges that are comfortable to wear. Settings that hold. Proportions that look right on a real hand, not just in a photograph. It's also why she still works the traditional way, sawing, filing, soldering and polishing every piece herself in her Cape Town studio.

Studio Calypso is the home for that work: custom pieces designed together with you, a small collection of studio favourites, and workshops where she shares what she knows with anyone curious enough to pick up a saw.

Silver miniatures

Small things, made properly

Alongside her jewellery, Hestelle is known among collectors for her 1:12 scale silver miniatures: tea sets, gravy boats, candy dishes and keepsake boxes, some no taller than a fingernail. One of her silver chalices stands just nine millimetres high.

Her miniatures are collected around the world and stocked by specialist miniature boutiques. They have been taught as class projects at miniature conventions and have come up for auction at Strauss & Co.

It's exacting work, and the same patience and precision go into every ring she makes.

How the studio works

One maker. No shortcuts.

Studio Calypso is deliberately small. That's what makes it special, and it's also why there's sometimes a wait.

Made by one pair of hands

No production line and no outsourcing. Every piece is designed, made and finished by Hestelle at her own bench.

Honest, fair pricing

Boutique shouldn't mean unaffordable. You'll get a clear quote up front, with options to suit your budget, whether that's silver, gold, or re-using metal you already own.

Worth the wait

Because she works alone, Hestelle takes on a limited number of commissions. Right now that means around 10–12 weeks, and she'll always be straight with you about timing.
