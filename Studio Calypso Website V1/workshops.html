Good to know

Workshop questions

Do I need any jewellery-making experience?

Not at all. The Silver Jewellery and Couples' Ring workshops are designed for complete beginners. Hestelle demonstrates every step and works alongside you. The miniature workshop is open to beginners too, though some making experience helps.

How many people are in a workshop?

Never more than four guests, so you get plenty of one-on-one time and room to work at your own pace.

Where are the workshops held?

In Hestelle's working studio in Cape Town. The exact address and parking details are shared when your booking is confirmed.

How do I book and pay?

Send an enquiry with your preferred dates. Once a date is confirmed, a deposit secures your seat. Dates usually book up around 3–4 weeks ahead, so it's worth getting in touch early.

What should I wear?

Closed shoes, hair tied back and clothes you don't mind getting a little dusty. Aprons, safety glasses and everything else are provided.

Can I buy a workshop as a gift?

Yes. Gift vouchers are available for every workshop and are valid for 12 months. Request a voucher.
