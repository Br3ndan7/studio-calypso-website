Good to know

Commission questions

How long does a custom piece take?

Commissions currently take around 10–12 weeks from design approval, because Hestelle makes every piece herself. If you have a date in mind, such as a proposal, wedding or birthday, mention it when you enquire and she'll plan around it where she can.

Do you make engagement rings and wedding bands?

Yes, and they're some of the most special pieces Hestelle makes. She'll work with your budget, help you choose a stone, and can design wedding bands to sit perfectly alongside an existing engagement ring.

I want it to be a surprise. What if I don't know their ring size?

Very common! Hestelle can help you estimate a size discreetly (borrowing a ring they wear on the right finger is the classic trick), and most rings can be resized after the big moment.

Can you use my own gold or stones?

Often, yes. Remodelling an heirloom, whether that's re-using a grandmother's gold or re-setting a family stone, is a lovely way to keep something meaningful in everyday wear. Bring the piece to your consultation and Hestelle will advise.

Can I buy one of the pieces on the site?

Of course. Choose "A piece I saw on the site" in the form above and tell us which one. If it has sold out, it can usually be made to order.
